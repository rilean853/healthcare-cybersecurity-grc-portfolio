# Healthcare Cybersecurity Risk Assessment

**Scenario:** Fictional 50-person outpatient healthcare organization.

## Objective
Identify cybersecurity risks affecting patient information, clinical operations, business systems, and supporting infrastructure.

## Methodology
1. Identify critical assets and business processes.
2. Identify threats and vulnerabilities.
3. Evaluate likelihood and impact.
4. Assign inherent risk.
5. Identify existing controls.
6. Determine residual risk.
7. Recommend treatment actions.
8. Assign owners and target dates.

## Key Deliverables
- Risk register
- Risk treatment plan
- Executive summary

## Sample Findings
- MFA not consistently enforced for workforce accounts.
- Vendor security reviews are informal.
- Endpoint patching is not measured against defined SLAs.
- Backup recovery testing is inconsistent.
- Excessive access exists for selected shared accounts.

All findings are made up to suite the projects.
